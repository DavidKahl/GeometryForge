# Astraeus Heavy Lander — printable interpretation

Based on the concept image supplied in chat on 3 October 2026. This is a new procedural interpretation for a display model.

The concept specifies a 72 m height, 8.5 m hull, 13 m aft-fin span, 14.5 m deployed-leg span, 6.2 m engine cluster and 1.4 m engine bells. Section lengths are 12.5 m aft, 34 m tank, 10 m cargo and 15.5 m nose. Nominal scale is 1:150: 480 mm tall with a 56.67 mm hull. The 6 m upper-canard label cannot describe the entire span across an 8.5 m hull; canard proportions therefore follow the drawing's silhouette.

Retained visual features: long stainless-colored hull, circumferential weld seams, longitudinal panel lines, recessed service hatches and raised latches, pointed ogive with separate dark tip, four swept canards, four large reinforced aft fins, integral hex-tile relief on a separate dark thermal sleeve, seven hollow engine bells with lip/cooling-ring details, engine bulkhead, and four deployed landing legs with lightening openings, piston relief and wide feet.

Print adaptations: hollow segmented hull, 2.4 mm nominal walls, stout separate fins, fixed deployed legs, minimum practical small features rather than literal microscopic rivets, slip-fit collars and rectangular attachment tabs. Small surface marks and hatches are interpreted rather than claiming an exact match to unreadable image detail. No articulated mechanisms or working propulsion.

The user confirmed a 480 mm model, Bambu X2D, 0.4 mm nozzle and 256 mm bed. A conservative 250 mm part-height limit is used for geometry checks. Suggested layers are 0.12–0.16 mm. Fit clearance is 0.30 mm per side; print the included male/female coupon before the model. Meshing and fit validation do not replace a real test print.
