# Code of conduct

GeometryForge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful. Assume good intent, give and take constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the people using and building this project. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub ([@DavidKahl](https://github.com/DavidKahl)) or by email to the address on that profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful, following the covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
